Stock Price Calculator

Convert stock purchases to AUD, work out the cost of a single item from a bulk pack, and calculate vending and ticket prices.

01

Purchase details

Enter the total amount paid for the stock.

Item name

Use a descriptive name to identify the stock item in the results. This field is optional and does not affect the price.

Total purchase price

Enter the total amount paid for the item or full pack, before splitting by quantity.

For example, if a pack of five costs $20, enter 20 here and 5 under quantity.

$
Purchase currency

Choose the currency you were charged in. AUD is the default.

USD, GBP and EUR are converted into AUD before calculating prices. You can adjust the exchange rate if needed.

Shipping cost

Enter the shipping charged for this entire purchase or pack, in the same currency as the purchase price.

Shipping is added once to the purchase total, converted to AUD if necessary, and divided across all items in the pack.

Leave this at zero if shipping is free or already included in the purchase price.

$
Pack quantity

Enter how many individual stock items come in the full purchase.

For a single item enter 1. A $20 pack containing 5 items costs $4 per item before pricing multipliers.

ITEMS

Currency conversion

Checking exchange rate…

Exchange rate

How many Australian dollars equal one unit of the selected foreign currency.

For example, if 1 USD equals 1.50 AUD, enter 1.50. The rate loads automatically when available, but you can edit it.

Use your actual bank or supplier rate if it differs from the reference rate.

All displayed AUD prices are rounded to the nearest 10 cents. Tickets are rounded to the nearest 10 below 500, nearest 50 from 500 to 2,500, and nearest 100 above 2,500. Calculations use the unrounded per-item cost.

HOW IT WORKS

The calculation

01 / CONVERT

Purchase price in AUD

(Purchase total + shipping) × AUD exchange rate

Shipping is entered in the purchase currency and added before conversion. AUD purchases have a conversion rate of 1.

02 / SPLIT

Single-item purchase cost

Total landed cost in AUD Pack quantity

For a single item, leave the quantity set to 1.

03 / PRICE

Vending and ticket prices

Vending = Unit cost × 1.30 → nearest $0.10
Tickets = Unit cost × 165 → ticket tier rounding

Both values are based on the unrounded AUD cost of one item. Ticket rounding uses the original calculated ticket amount to choose the tier.
