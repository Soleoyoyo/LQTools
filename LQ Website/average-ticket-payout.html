Average Ticket Payout Calculator

Record how many tickets you win each time you test a game. Calculate the average tickets per play and the ticket payout percentage relative to the play price.

01

Test results

Enter the price of one play, then record each result.

Game name

Enter the name of the arcade game you are testing.

This appears at the top of your exported PDF report.

Price of play

Enter the amount charged for one game, in Australian dollars.

If a game costs $2.00 per play, enter 2. This is used to calculate ticket payout percentage.

$ AUD
Number of test plays

How many times you tested the game. One ticket-entry row appears for every play.

You can change this number or use the + Add Play button to record more tests.

TICKETS WON PER PLAY

Tickets won per play

Enter the actual number of tickets awarded on each test run, including zero if no tickets were won.

The average is the total tickets won divided by the number of test plays.
