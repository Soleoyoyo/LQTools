RECOMMENDED GAME SETTING
What does this setting mean?

This is the target average number of paid plays needed for the machine to award a prize from the loaded prize pool.

For example, a setting of 12 means the game is configured to award approximately one prize for every 12 plays on average.

It does not guarantee a prize on exactly the 12th play. Actual results depend on the machine's settings and gameplay.
